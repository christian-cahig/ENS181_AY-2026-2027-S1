## Item 01

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-d\beta + d\alpha = 0$$
or
$$\frac{d\alpha}{d\beta} = 1$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable, linear, homogeneous (of degree 0), and exact.

## Item 02

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$v^{3} dv + u^{3} du = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable, homogeneous (of degree 3), and exact.

## Item 03

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-S dR + R dS = 0$$
or
$$\frac{dS}{dR} - \frac{1}{R}S = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable, linear, and homogeneous (of degree 1).

## Item 04

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-t dt + dw = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable, linear, and exact.

## Item 05

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( \theta - 2\phi \right) d\phi + \phi d\theta = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
linear, homogeneous (of degree 1), and exact.

## Item 06

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-n^{2} dm + m^{2} dn = 0$$
or
$$\frac{dn}{dm} = \frac{n^{2}}{m^{2}}$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable and homogeneous (of degree 2).

## Item 07

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$3q^{2} dq + 2p dp = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable and exact.

## Item 08

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-KT dT + dK = 0$$
or
$$\frac{dK}{dT} - TK = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable and linear.

## Item 09

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( u^{2} - v^{2} \right) dv + 2uv du = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
homogeneous (of degree 2) and exact.

## Item 10

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( B - A \right) dB + B dA = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
linear and homogeneous (of degree 1).

## Item 11

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( 2rs + \cos s \right) ds + s^{2} dr = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
linear and exact.

## Item 12

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$-e^{w} \sin z dz + dw = 0$$
or
$$e^{-w} dw = \sin z dz$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable.

## Item 13

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( q^{2} - pq \right) dp + p^{2} dq = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
homogeneous (of degree 2).

## Item 14

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( \lambda^{2} + \cos \omega \right) d\omega + 2\lambda \omega d\lambda = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
exact.

## Item 15

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( \Gamma \sin\Psi - \Psi^{2} \cos\Psi \right) d\Psi + \cos\Psi d\Gamma = 0$$
or
$$\frac{d\Gamma}{d\Psi} + \Gamma\tan\Psi = \Psi^{2}$$

Therefore, the ODE is
linear.

## Item 16

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\frac{d\eta}{d\xi} = \eta^{2} + \xi^{2} + 1$$

Therefore, the ODE is
non-separable, non-homogeneous, not exact, and nonlinear.
This is a Riccati equation.

## Item 17

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\frac{dp}{dq} = \ln\left(p + q\right)$$

Therefore, the ODE is
non-separable, non-homogeneous, not exact, and nonlinear.

## Item 18

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$3c^{2} \Delta dc + c^{3} d\Delta = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
separable, linear, homogeneous (of degree 3), and exact.

## Item 19

The ODE can be rewritten as:
$$\left( a e^{b} + e^{a} \right) db + \left( e^{b} + b e^{a} \right) da = 0$$

Therefore, the ODE is
exact.

## Item 20

$$-K^{2} dK + \left( J^{2} + 1 \right) dJ = 0$$
